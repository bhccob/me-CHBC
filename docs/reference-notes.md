# Reference notes for the new opening

Looked at in the Browser pane on 2026-11-10, at desktop size, scrolling each from the top. Sound was left off; no cookie banner came up. These are notes on what each site does and the principle behind it. Nothing here is to be copied: not a shape, a layout, a loader or a line.

## jesperlandberg.com (the benchmark for feel)

- **First paint:** black, then three short white dashes in the middle as the loader. They resolve into a black space with a fine perspective grid floor. Four tiny labels in the corners (name, Profile, Featured / Full, Newsletter). Nothing else.
- **Scroll:** the works are large pictures standing in the dark, arranged on a curve. They swing in from the right, bend into a curved band with the scroll's speed and settle flat when it stops. Titles sit in small type at their feet.
- **Feel:** inertia. The scroll is never 1 to 1: the scene catches up a beat late and settles without bounce. Speed shows in the picture (bend), not in a counter.
- **Principle:** one space, one material (black and light), the motion itself is the content. The type is small because the pictures are huge.

## kinoria.studio

- **Loader:** belongs to the subject (a camera): a dim, out-of-focus scene with a framing overlay and a percent that counts up as part of the overlay.
- **Reveal:** focus resolves onto one object in warm, low film light on wood. Hard shadows, shallow depth of field, grain.
- **Scroll:** one hero object, then huge two-line type over it with a short dry line below.
- **Principle:** the loader is the first frame of the film, not a screen before it. One object, lit like a product shot in a dark room.

## portfolio-zxc.com

- **Loader:** a scrambled line of text that decodes, a hairline, a percent.
- **Gate:** clock faces and an eye texture with ENTER / PORTFOLIO and "enter without music".
- **Inside:** one object seen from above (a keyboard) inside a thin frame, warm lamp light, an interactions counter in the corner, a large word at the foot.
- **Principle:** the counter in the corner tells you there is more to find; the frame makes a 3D scene read as a composed picture.

## tenpoundfeather.com

- **First view:** cream paper, a thin rule frame, one engraved emblem, one instruction word.
- **Scroll:** the engraving becomes an extreme close-up of itself, then turns into a dark engraved machine of gears, all in the same line-engraving style.
- **Principle:** one drawing style for everything, so even a big change of subject reads as one world.

## jjettas.com

- **Loader:** the mark, filling from grey to white, with a percent under it.
- **Scroll:** type used as architecture (huge letters behind and in front of the subject), sharp diagonal cuts between sections, a yard-line motif that ties sections together.
- **Principle:** a motif from the subject's own world (here the field's yard lines) carries the layout.

## lorenzolottici.it

- **Loader:** a near-black screen with a thin line, then LISTEN / SILENT. Scattered dark cubes arrange themselves into a grid as it loads: the loader is the site's idea (order out of chaos).
- **First view:** a dark sea at night, a moon, the cubes as one structure, big two-line headline at the left, small labels in the corners.
- **Scroll:** the structure breaks and re-forms; a ribbon of pictures twists through the scene.
- **Principle:** one sentence for the whole site, acted out by the scene from the loader on.

## What they share, and what that means for Bob's opening

1. **One idea you can say in a sentence**, acted out from the loader to the last frame. For Bob: a machine is mechanics, electronics and software working as one; the opening is one chain where each part causes the next.
2. **The loader is the first frame of the world**, styled as part of it. For Bob: an instrument readout (a supply's volts, a placement count, a dimension line), never a spinner.
3. **Weight in the scroll.** The picture follows the scroll a beat late and settles. For Bob: the site's own eased frame (0.13 s), one clock for the opening and the brain.
4. **Big, confident type, few words.** Huge display type, a mono for small labels, nothing in between that competes.
5. **One hero object, lit like film.** One warm key, a cool or violet fill, deep shadows, shallow focus, grain.
6. **Nothing on screen without a reason.** Corner labels are the only chrome.

## Not to take

No viewfinder or camera framing, no keyboard, no paper pulled away, no cube field or ribbon, no pictures bending on a curve, no clock faces or eyes, no yard lines. Bob's own motifs are the BC-2006 mark, the gold of his site, its dark purple, and the machine.
