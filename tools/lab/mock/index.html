Opening mock-ups

Browser mock-ups made before any build: the coin from Bob's brief (11 Nov 2026), and two more ideas in the same spirit. Click a frame to open it live; the first view reacts to the cursor, and its O turns when you hover or drag it. Stills come from tools/lab/mock/shoot.mjs.

A · The coin

Bob's brief. The O in BOB is a Chinese coin: Built on one face, Studies on the other, the square hole is mechatronics. Robot hands catch it and feed it to a visor; it ends on Bob's own monitor.

B · The seal

The full stop after CHUNG is a square of seal paste. It lifts: a stone seal carved in circuit traces with BC 2006, one study on each side, the knob still uncarved (mechatronics). Each project is a print it stamps on the page.

C · The cartridge

The first view is a poster, and it is a label. Pulling back shows a game cartridge; inside, four chips are the studies and one socket is still empty. It plays in the console under Bob's monitor at the end.
